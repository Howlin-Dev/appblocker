## 2. Special Permissions
To provide its core functionality, Bloq requires high-level system permissions. By using the App, you acknowledge and agree to the following:

### 2.1 Accessibility Service
The App uses the Android **AccessibilityService API** to:
- Detect when a "blocked" application is being launched to prevent its access.
- Inspect browser URL bars to identify and block specified websites.
**Note:** Bloq does not use this service to collect or share any personal or sensitive user data. All processing happens locally on your device.

### 2.2 Notification Access
The App uses **NotificationListenerService** to read and suppress notifications from apps you have chosen to block. This is necessary to prevent distractions during your focus sessions.

### 2.3 System Overlay (Display over other apps)
This permission allows the App to display the "Blocked" screen overlay on top of other applications when they are intercepted.

### 2.4 Usage Access
This permission is used to identify the currently running application and provide accurate blocking functionality.

### 2.5 Battery Optimization
To ensure reliable blocking and scheduling, you may be asked to exempt the App from system battery optimizations. This prevents the Android system from killing the background services necessary for the App to function.

### 2.6 Exact Alarms
Used to schedule the precise start and end of your blocking profiles.

### 2.7 Other Permissions
- **Query All Packages**: Necessary to provide you with a list of installed applications so you can choose which ones to block.
- **Run at Startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED)**: Allows the App to resume its blocking and scheduling services automatically after your device restarts.
- **Post Notifications**: Used to show active profile status and timer information in your notification drawer.
